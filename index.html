How this is worked out

So you can argue with it

Each player gets a projected points-per-gameweek from three parts: 40% season scoring rate scaled by minutes, 30% recent form, and 30% underlying numbers — expected goals and assists, clean sheet odds from expected goals conceded, save rate for keepers, and defensive contributions. That's multiplied by injury availability, then by fixture difficulty over the window. Faded rows and "small sample" flags mean there isn't enough football played yet to trust the number. Only same-position swaps are suggested, since draft squads must stay legal at 2/5/5/3.
