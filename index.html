How the projection works

Currently 72% prior on average

Every player has a prior — what we'd expect before this season started — built from their points per 90 across the last three seasons (weighted toward the most recent), FPL's own draft rank, and set-piece duty. Penalty takers get a bump, as do first-choice corner and free-kick takers.
Against that sits the observed rate from this season: actual points per 90 blended with underlying numbers — expected goals and assists, clean-sheet odds from expected goals conceded, save rate for keepers, and defensive contributions against the position threshold.
The two combine according to how much football has actually been played: weight = minutes ÷ (minutes + 700). At 90 minutes played a player sits about 89% on the prior. At 700 minutes it's an even split. Playing time blends the same way with a smaller constant (320), because minutes settle faster than scoring does. The blue bar under each name shows how much of that player's number comes from this season rather than the prior.
Fixtures use each opponent's attack and defence strength ratings, home and away, rather than the blunt 1–5 difficulty score. Forwards and midfielders are weighted toward the opponent's defensive rating; keepers and defenders toward their attacking rating. Chips show the opponent — uppercase for home, lowercase for away.
Last-season history is cached in the repo and fetched once per player, so the first few builds fill it in gradually. Only same-position swaps are offered, since draft squads stay legal at 2/5/5/3.
